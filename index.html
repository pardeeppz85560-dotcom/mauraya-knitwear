OUR STORY

About Mauraya Knitwear

Quality winter knitwear made for everyday comfort.

Designed for warmth. Made for everyone.

Mauraya Knitwear is a winter sweater business based in Simlapuri, Ludhiana, Punjab. We focus on machine-made sweaters and winter knitwear for boys, girls, schools and colleges.

Our goal is to offer comfortable designs, useful everyday styles and dependable service for individual and bulk customers.
